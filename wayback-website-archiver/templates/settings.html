🔑 Internet Archive Credentials

Your S3 Access and Secret keys allow authenticated Save Page Now submissions with dedicated queue priority.

How to obtain your keys:
  1. Log in to your free account at archive.org.
  2. Navigate to https://archive.org/account/s3.php.
  3. Click "Generate New Keys" (or copy your existing S3 Access Key & Secret Key).
  4. Paste them below and click Test Connection.
Credentials are stored locally in data/settings.json and never shared or printed in reports.
